Philosophy Journey Part 2

Posted on: 2026-09-17

After completing four of the ten courses in my philosophy bachelor's program, I decided to move on to a master's degree. When Newlane announced its Master of Arts in Philosophy, a new graduate degree accredited by the U.S. Department of Education, I was thrilled to pursue it. Learn more about the program.

I already have a master's degree in computer science, but I want to dive even deeper into philosophy. The opportunity to begin another ten-course program felt like the right way to restart this journey with a new level of focus.

I am now starting the first course and look forward to sharing what I learn along the way.
